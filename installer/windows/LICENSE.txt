Copyright (c) LocalForge contributors. MIT License.

This installer ships the LocalForge companion (a local service that connects
the LocalForge IDE in your browser to your own files, terminals and AI model
servers) together with its tray application.

The companion runs entirely on YOUR computer:
- It binds to 127.0.0.1 (localhost) only — other machines cannot connect.
- Your files, prompts, tool results and inference traffic stay local.
- Nothing is installed besides the two executables in the install folder;
  settings and logs live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LocalForgeCompanion.

Third-party components are covered by their own licenses; the full list is in
the project repository (docs/LICENSES.md). The Bun runtime (MIT) is embedded
in localforge-companion.exe; the tray app is statically linked native code.
